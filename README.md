My idea is to build Skill Maps, an AI-powered learning and career assistant that helps students understand what they know and what they need to learn for their career goal. Students can connect their GitHub, LinkedIn, LeetCode, HackerRank, and other profiles using secure permission, and the AI will collect useful information about their skills, projects, experience, and learning activity. It will create an updated learner profile, find skill gaps, and suggest the right courses, projects, and assessments in the correct order. Students can tell the AI their goal in simple language, ask questions through the “I’m Confused” button, and get personalized advice from the AI mentor. The dashboard will stay simple and show their current goal, progress, next step, and weekly milestones. Every Sunday, the system will update the weekly learning goals based on their progress and feedback, so the learning path keeps changing according to the student's needs. For the prototype, user and profile data can be stored in Google Sheets.
